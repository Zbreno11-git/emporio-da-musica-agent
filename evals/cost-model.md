## 3. Monthly projection for the store

**Assumption:** 4 customer turns per conversation (not measured on real customers). Gemini = mean cost per turn × turns × conversations.

| Conversations / month | Gemini (`gemini-3.1-flash-lite`) | Cloud Run, gross (upper bound) | Cloud Run after the free tier | Total | Total (BRL) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 500 | US$ 4.06 | US$ 0.16 | US$ 0.05 | US$ 4.22 | R$ 22,89 |
| 2,000 | US$ 16.24 | US$ 0.65 | US$ 0.18 | US$ 16.89 | R$ 91,58 |
| 10,000 | US$ 81.19 | US$ 3.27 | US$ 0.92 | US$ 84.46 | R$ 457,88 |

Total uses Cloud Run **gross** (no free tier), the conservative figure.
Arithmetic check: US$ 0.002030 × 4 × 2,000 = US$ 16.24.

**Cloud Run line.** Request-based billing in `southamerica-east1` (Tier 2): US$ 0.0000336 per vCPU-second, US$ 0.0000035 per GiB-second and US$ 0.40 per million requests, with the deployed shape (1 vCPU, 0.5 GiB, min instances 0).
- Billable time per conversation = turns × mean turn latency (2.02 s) + 1 s for the page load.
- Requests = turns + 10 static/session requests.
- This is an **upper bound**: with concurrency 20, overlapping requests share billed instance time.
- The free tier (180,000 vCPU-s, 360,000 GiB-s and 2 million requests a month) belongs to the billing account and is shared with its other projects, so the gross figure is the safe one to budget.

## 6. Caveats

- 18 measured turns per model; the turn-type mix is the benchmark's, not the store's real traffic.
- Turns per conversation is an assumption; the sensitivity table shows how the cost moves with it.
- Prices are the list prices on the dates above; billing in BRL follows Google's SKU prices in BRL, which may differ slightly from the PTAX conversion.
- Excluded: Secret Manager and Artifact Registry storage (cents per month at this scale), network egress (small JSON and static files), and taxes.
